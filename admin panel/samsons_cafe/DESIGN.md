## Brand & Style
The design system embodies the sensory intimacy and quiet luxury of an ultra-premium artisanal coffee house. Designed for discerning patrons and high-velocity boutique cafe operations, the experience feels warm, deliberate, and uncompromisingly refined.

The visual direction combines **Warm Minimalist Luxury** with **Translucent Tactile Depth**:
- **Tonal Submersion:** Ultra-deep roasted-bean obsidian blacks layered over dark mahogany undertones create an intimate, ambient lounge environment.
- **Antique Metallic Lustre:** Burnished gold and champagne accents bring tactile prestige without ostentation, serving strictly as luminous focal points for action and key metadata.
- **Editorial Legibility:** Crisp, ivory-set serif headlines communicate heritage and craftsmanship, anchored by high-clarity geometric sans-serifs for frictionless transactional velocity.
- **Effortless Commerce:** Interface density is calibrated for rapid, two-tap mobile ordering while retaining generous spatial rhythm, understated glass surfaces, and refined brass-wire borders.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in the depth of dark espresso roast, patinated metals, and warm porcelain crema.

### Core Swatches
- **Canvas Base (`#0B0806`):** Deep roasted obsidian for page backdrops, full-bleed modals, and underlying scrims.
- **Elevated Canvas (`#140F0B`):** Secondary stage tone for navigation headers, sheets, and grouped view backings.
- **Container Surfaces (`#1A1410` & `#221B15`):** Warm espresso panels and card tiers, maintaining natural organic warmth rather than cold gray slate.
- **Primary Accent (`#D4AF37` / `#E5C158` / `#B8860B`):** Metallic burnished gold reserved for primary interactions, state fills, critical status indicators, and selected triggers.
- **Hairline Accent (`#C8A251` / `#9E7D3B`):** Antique brass tones diluted to 20%–40% alpha for razor-sharp hairline framing and separation rules.
- **Typography & Content:**
  - **Cream Light (`#FBF7EE`):** Primary display text, hero headings, and prominent numerals.
  - **Ivory Neutral (`#E6DEC8`):** Body copy, default labels, and interactive icons.
  - **Warm Muted Ash (`#A89F91`):** Subtext, descriptors, timestamps, and inactive iconography.

### Status Indicators
- **Hot Badge:** `#C25438` (Smoked terracotta red) with `#FDF0ED` text.
- **Cold Badge:** `#4E7A8A` (Deep Nordic slate-cyan) with `#F0F8FA` text.
- **Available / Success:** `#8A9A5B` (Muted sage bronze) with `#F4F7EE` text.
- **Low Stock / Alert:** `#D4AF37` (Primary antique gold) with dark espresso text.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm establishes the dialogue between heritage cafe warmth and modern transactional efficiency.

- **Display & Product Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Applied exclusively to the cafe masthead, category divisions, beverage names, tasting profiles, and numerical prices. Playfair Display delivers classical elegance with high-contrast serifs, elevating coffee selection into a boutique experience.
- **Functional Body & Interface (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Applied to ingredient lists, roast levels, order configuration toggles, cart lists, metadata badges, and administrative data tables. Its geometric structure ensures absolute legibility against dark roasted container backgrounds.
- **Stylistic Treatments:**
  - Badges, category subheads, and stock states utilize uppercase styling with generous tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) via `label-sm` and `label-md`.
  - Item currency symbols are set slightly smaller and vertically centered beside Playfair Display price numerals for clean baseline alignment.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout balances spacious, magazine-like storytelling with compact, high-efficiency ordering modules.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (Base to 639px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Content flows vertically with compact card heights to maintain rapid single-thumb interaction. Bottom of viewport is preserved for a fixed `64px` floating glass navigation bar.
- **Tablet (640px to 1023px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins. Menu catalogs reorganize into a responsive dual-column grid with sticky side drawers for ongoing order receipts.
- **Desktop (1024px and above):** 12-column layout capped at an inner maximum width of `1280px` with `3rem` margins. Supports split-screen luxury layouts: a persistent sensory product showcase on the left, alongside interactive ordering menus and admin tables on the right.

### Vertical Cadence
- Standard structural rhythm adheres strictly to an `8px` scale.
- Card padding remains tight (`space-md` / `1rem`) for order catalog items to allow maximum menu visibility, expanding to `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) on modal order customizers and checkout summaries.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is constructed through physical light interaction, tonal density, and translucent glass tiers rather than stark drop shadows.

- **Surface Tier 0 (Canvas):** Pure base (`#0B0806`), flat and light-absorbent.
- **Surface Tier 1 (Base Cards & Shelves):** `#140F0B` with a `1px` subtle outline of `rgba(200, 162, 81, 0.15)`. No drop shadow.
- **Surface Tier 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Controls):** `#1A1410` paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a hairline border of `rgba(200, 162, 81, 0.3)`. Grounded by an ambient undertone shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Surface Tier 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Nav):** `#1A1410` at 85% opacity, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, crowned with a top rim specular highlight (`rgba(229, 193, 88, 0.25)`) and deep environmental drop: `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`.
- **Glow Accents:** Focused active items (such as the quick-add circular button on hover or active filter chips) project a subtle radial gold radiance: `0 0 16px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`.

## Shapes
A conservative roundedness value (`1` - Soft) maintains architectural gravitas and artisanal restraint. 

- **Containers & Panels:** Cards, sheet drawers, banners, and modals employ `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`) corner radii, preserving sharp, structured luxury without harsh raw brutishness.
- **Interactive Controls:** Form text fields, quantity steppers, and pill-shaped badges follow tailored exceptions:
  - Form inputs and category chips use `0.375rem` to `0.5rem` to anchor structural stability.
  - Quick-add circular buttons (`+`) and counter incrementors override to absolute circles (`border-radius: 9999px`) to invite effortless fingertip precision.
- **Badges:** Tight `0.25rem` micro-radii to echo tailored luggage tags or brass label inserts.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Add to Order", "Proceed to Checkout"):**
  Solid burnished gold gradient (`from-[#E5C158] to-[#B8860B]`) with `#0B0806` heavy espresso sans-serif typography (`label-lg`). Subtly shines on hover with an internal specular gold line (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.3)`).
- **Secondary / Action Outlines:**
  Transparent background with a hairline border of `rgba(200, 162, 81, 0.4)`, `#FBF7EE` text. Upon hover, shifts to `rgba(200, 162, 81, 0.1)` background and `#D4AF37` border.
- **Circular Quick-Add Button (`+`):**
  A tactile `40px` circular button (`rounded-full`) positioned at card base. Default: `#221B15` background with `1px` border of `rgba(200, 162, 81, 0.35)` and gold glyph. Press/hover: snaps instantly to solid gold fill (`#D4AF37`) with obsidian glyph for immediate tactile feedback.

### Product Cards
- Built on `#1A1410` glass panels with a continuous `1px` gold hairline frame (`rgba(200, 162, 81, 0.2)`).
- Card contains an imagery container ratio of `4:3` or `1:1`, followed by beverage name in `headline-sm` (`Playfair Display`), concise tasting notes in muted ash, a bold serif price, and the floating circular quick-add icon.
- Cards maintain zero layout shift on mobile tap, executing the two-click ordering paradigm (Tap 1: Default added to cart; Tap-and-hold/Card-body tap: Opens configuration drawer).

### Badges & Micro-Labels
- Compact micro-tags utilizing `label-sm` tracking.
- Styled with dark translucent backgrounds matching status hues, framed by a delicate `1px` border (e.g., `HOT` uses terracotta background tint `rgba(194, 84, 56, 0.15)` with `rgba(194, 84, 56, 0.4)` border).
- `LOW STOCK` employs antique gold pulse styling: `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)` fill with pure metallic gold text.

### Chips & Filter Tabs
- Used for roast profiles, milk choices, and category filtering (Espresso, Pour Over, Cold Brew, Pastries).
- Inactive: `#140F0B` with muted ivory text and `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` border.
- Active: `#221B15` base with a distinct `1px` metallic gold border (`#D4AF37`), gold text (`#E5C158`), and a faint inner ambient amber glow.

### Input Fields & Steppers
- Dark container fill (`#140F0B`) with an inset hairline border (`rgba(200, 162, 81, 0.25)`). Focus states illuminate the border to solid `#D4AF37` with an ambient glow, eliminating harsh browser rings.
- Placeholder text is set in `#A89F91` at 60% opacity.
- Numeric steppers for milk quantity, syrup shots, and cups feature circular brass-wire minus/plus controls flanking ivory serif figures.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Radios are circular brass rings; selection renders an inner solid gold medallion dot (`#D4AF37`).
- Checkboxes feature a soft-square brass outline (`rounded-[3px]`), filling with solid gold and an obsidian checkmark when chosen.

### Fixed Mobile Bottom Navigation
- Persistent `64px` high floating bar hovering `12px` above screen bottom, spanning canvas width minus `2rem`.
- Features an ultra-dark brown frosted backdrop (`rgba(20, 15, 11, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`), enclosed by an antique gold hairline border (`rgba(200, 162, 81, 0.25)`).
- Accommodates fast switching between Menu, Search, Orders, and a highlighted Cart trigger containing a real-time badge count.

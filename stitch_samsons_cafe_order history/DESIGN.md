## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an intimate, sensory-rich digital atmosphere akin to an exclusive private coffee salon and culinary atelier after dusk. It balances uncompromising luxury with effortless transactional utility. 

The aesthetic is anchored in **Dark Luxury Minimalism with Tactile Warmth**. It eschews sterile technology in favor of rich espresso tones, burnished metallic gold trimmings, deep charcoal shadows, and luminous warm cream typography. Every interaction feels deliberate, calm, and artisanal. 

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Connoisseurs of specialty coffee, fine dining patrons, and design-conscious urban professionals seeking frictionless, elevated ordering.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Quiet indulgence, prestige, artisanal mastery, and comforting warmth. Interfaces should evoke the tactile sensations of heavy stoneware, oiled walnut counters, and the polished brass valves of a bespoke espresso machine.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an immersive, low-light environment punctuated by precious metallic accents and readable ivory copy.

### Palette Architecture
- **Base Canvas (`#0B0908`):** Deep obsidian-black, evoking the quiet ambience of an evening lounge.
- **Surface & Panels (`#120E0C` & `#1C1512`):** Rich roasted coffee grounds providing organic depth across containers, drawers, and card tiers.
- **Elevated Surfaces (`#261E1A`):** Roasted walnut tone reserved for highlighted states, hover surfaces, and floating navigation bars.
- **Primary Antique Gold (`#C8A261`):** Subdued, timeless gold used for primary callouts, active indicators, and delicate hairline frames.
- **Secondary & Accent Gold (`#E5C158` / `#D4AF37`):** Brilliant liquid metal reserved for micro-accents, quantity adjustments (`+`), badges, and focal status glows.
- **Muted Structural Taupe (`#3D312A` & `#5A473D`):** Quiet boundaries that articulate space without introducing high-contrast grid lines.
- **Typographic Cream (`#FAF6F0` & `#F5EBE1`):** Warm ivory replacing stark white to eliminate eye strain while maintaining impeccable contrast over dark espresso layers. Secondary text defaults to a dimmed latte foam (`#A8988B`).

## Typography

The typographical treatment marries the editorial romance of high-contrast display serif typography with the clinical clarity of a modernist geometric grotesque.

### Typographic Roles
- **Playfair Display:** Governs the narrative, menu categories, hero statements, and item titles. It brings bespoke heritage and classical grandeur to the digital cafe menu.
- **Outfit:** Engineered for ordering clarity. It is utilized across culinary notes, dietary flags, operational timestamps, micro-labels, and currency counters. Its geometric precision guarantees that pricing, portions, and ingredients are legible at a glance.
- **Tracking Discipline:** Display and headline levels feature tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `0em`) for a curated editorial posture. All micro-labels, tags, badges, and uppercase category ribbons employ wide tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to command presence without visual bulk.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout delivers a spacious, desktop-first boutique experience with an upper bound of `1440px`, transitioning cleanly across viewports.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Desktop (1440px max-width):** 12-column symmetrical fluid grid. Outer margins are anchored at `3rem` (`48px`) with gutters set to `1.5rem` (`24px`). Menu layouts use asymmetrical groupings (e.g., 4-column category sidebar, 8-column product grid).
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column grid with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. The category navigation transitions to a sticky top horizontal rail.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Product cards reflow to single-column full-width or tight dual-column editorial stacks.

### Spacing Philosophy
Negative space is treated with architectural significance. Generous internal card padding (`space-lg`) prevents dense clustering, allowing product photography, antique gold typography, and price tags room to breathe.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is conveyed through roasted tonal stratification, low-contrast metallic edges, and ambient warm-tinted shadow plumes. Traditional drop shadows are replaced by warm, atmospheric luminescence.

### Elevation Architecture
- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#0B0908`.
- **Layer 1 (Cards & Sidebars):** Background `#120E0C` enclosed by a razor-thin 1px border of `#3D312A`. 
- **Layer 2 (Floating Cart / Overlays / Active Cards):** Background `#1C1512` elevated by an ambient shadow (`0 20px 40px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(200, 162, 97, 0.25)`).
- **Layer 3 (Modals & Tasting Drawers):** Background `#1C1512` with a 1px full perimeter stroke of Antique Gold (`#C8A261`, 30% opacity) and deep backdrop falloff (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` over dark tinted veil).

### Gold Illumination
Hovered interactive elements cast an ambient, non-distracting halo:
- `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(200, 162, 97, 0.22);`

## Shapes

The geometric silhouette combines softened architectural corners with precision circles, balancing classic bistro luxury with modern tactile software.

### Geometry Breakdown
- **Standard Containers & Product Cards:** Rounded level `2` (`0.5rem` / `8px` to `1rem` / `16px` for outer cards). Softened enough to feel welcoming, but sharp enough to retain tailoring and rigor.
- **Micro Tags & Origin Badges:** Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to offset rectangular photography and content blocks.
- **Primary Stepper Buttons & Modifiers:** Perfect circles (`aspect-ratio: 1/1; border-radius: 50%`) designed as jewel-like tactile coins.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Controls
- **Primary CTA ("Add to Tasting Order"):** Solid antique gold background (`#C8A261`), dark espresso bold typography (`#0B0908`), subtle brushed gold top inner bevel (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`). On hover, shifts to metallic gold (`#E5C158`) with a gentle warm bloom.
- **Circular Stepper Buttons (`+` / `-`):** 36px circles with a solid `#261E1A` body, encased in a 1px `#C8A261` boundary. The plus glyph is rendered in radiant gold (`#E5C158`). Hover triggers an inverted fill of solid antique gold with dark coffee icon glyphs.

### Menu Item & Product Cards
- **Structure:** Dark espresso surface (`#120E0C`), 1px outline in muted taupe (`#3D312A`). 
- **Imagery:** High-contrast, top-anchored food/drink visuals with a subtle vignette fade that melts into the card background.
- **Interactive State:** Hover elevates card background to `#1C1512`, borders brighten to antique gold (`#C8A261` at 60% opacity), and the card subtly translates -2px vertically.

### Chips & Origin Pills
- Small status labels ("Single Origin", "House Roast", "Gluten Free") styled with a translucent espresso backdrop (`rgba(38, 30, 26, 0.7)`), 1px gold hairline frame (`rgba(200, 162, 97, 0.35)`), and tracked uppercase gold/cream typography (`label-sm`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#0B0908` inset, framed in `#3D312A`. Placeholder in muted roast taupe (`#A8988B`). Active focus transitions the border to antique gold (`#C8A261`) with a restrained 2px radial glow.

### Checkboxes & Customization Toggles (Milk, Syrups, Roasts)
- Radio & checkbox controls are custom 18px rounded squares or circles in dark brown (`#1C1512`) bounded by `#5A473D`. Selected state displays a central gold pip (`#E5C158`) with a delicate illuminated halo.

### Specialized Component: Order Folio (Drawer / Sticky Bar)
- Persistent order summary drawer finished in brushed deep obsidian (`#120E0C`) with a top border in brushed gold metallic hairline. Price totals set in high-contrast `Playfair Display` in warm ivory (`#FAF6F0`).
